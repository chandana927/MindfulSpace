About This Technique

Calms the nervous system. Great for anxiety & sleep.

Inhale 4s Hold 7s Exhale 8s

Best Practices

Find a comfortable sitting position. Keep your back straight but relaxed. Close your eyes or soften your gaze. Breathe in through your nose and out through your mouth.

When to use?

When you feel overwhelmed, before an exam, or when you are having trouble falling asleep.
